## 1. LEAVE POLICIES

---

### 1.1 Sick Leave (SL)

**Purpose:** To provide employees with paid time off when they are unable to work due to illness or medical conditions.

**Entitlement:**
- All full-time permanent employees are entitled to **10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year**.
- Part-time employees receive sick leave on a **pro-rata basis** calculated against their contracted hours.
- Sick leave is **non-cumulative** — unused days do not carry forward to the next year.
- Sick leave **cannot be encashed** under any circumstances.

**Procedure:**
- The employee must **notify their direct manager and HR** via email or phone **before 9:30 AM** on the day of absence.
- For absences of **1–2 days**, self-declaration through the HR portal is sufficient.
- For absences of **3 or more consecutive days**, a **registered medical certificate** from a licensed physician must be submitted within 2 working days of return.
- For absences exceeding **7 consecutive days**, the employee must submit a detailed medical report and may be asked to undergo a fitness-to-work assessment before resuming duties.

**Scenarios:**
| Scenario | Action Required |
|----------|----------------|
| 1 day sick leave | Notify manager + portal entry |
| 2 days sick leave | Notify manager + portal entry |
| 3–6 days sick leave | Medical certificate on return |
| 7+ days sick leave | Detailed medical report + HR review |
| Sick during approved annual leave | Sick leave may replace annual leave with valid medical proof |
| Sick leave on a public holiday | Not counted; public holiday stands |

---

### 1.2 Casual Leave (CL)

**Purpose:** To allow employees to attend to unforeseen personal matters or emergencies.

**Entitlement:**
- All permanent employees are entitled to **7 days of casual leave per calendar year**.
- Casual leave is **not cumulative** and lapses at year end.
- A maximum of **3 consecutive casual leave days** can be taken at one time.
- Casual leave **cannot be combined** with annual leave without prior HR approval.

**Procedure:**
- Casual leave must be applied through the **HR portal at least 24 hours in advance** for planned absences.
- For emergencies, the employee must **notify the manager within 2 hours** of the start of business.
- **Manager approval is mandatory** before the leave is considered sanctioned.
- Unapproved casual leave will be treated as **Leave Without Pay (LWP)**.

**Scenarios:**
| Scenario | Policy |
|----------|--------|
| Emergency same-day leave | Notify manager within 2 hours |
| Planned personal event | Apply 24 hrs in advance via portal |
| Taking more than 3 consecutive days | Not permitted under casual leave |
| Used all 7 CL days and needs more | Apply for Annual Leave or LWP |

---

### 1.3 Annual Leave / Earned Leave (AL/EL)

**Purpose:** To allow employees to rest, travel, and rejuvenate.

**Entitlement:**
- Employees earn **1.5 days of annual leave per month**, totaling **18 days per year** after completing a minimum of 6 months of service.
- Employees in their **first 6 months receive no annual leave** but begin accruing from Day 1.
- Unused annual leave can be **carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days** into the next year. Any excess lapses.
- Up to **10 days of annual leave** can be **encashed** once per year during the annual review cycle.

**Procedure:**
- Annual leave must be applied through the **HR portal at least 5 working days in advance**.
- For leaves exceeding **10 working days**, a **minimum of 3 weeks' advance notice** is required.
- Approval is at the **manager's discretion** and depends on business requirements.
- During project critical phases or peak business periods, leave may be **deferred** by the manager.

**Scenarios:**
| Scenario | Policy |
|----------|--------|
| Employee in first 3 months | No AL; begins accruing |
| 5-day vacation | Apply 5 working days in advance |
| 15-day international trip | Apply 3 weeks in advance |
| 40 days accrued at year end | 30 days carried forward; 10 days lapse |
| Requesting encashment | One-time per year; max 10 days |
| Leave during probation | Not permitted |

---

### 1.4 Maternity Leave

**Purpose:** To support employees before and after childbirth.

**Entitlement:**
- Female employees who have completed **6 months of continuous service** are entitled to **26 weeks (182 days) of paid maternity leave** for the first two live births.
- For the **third child onwards**, maternity leave is limited to **12 weeks (84 days)** paid.
- **Adoption leave:** Employees adopting a child below the age of 3 months are entitled to **12 weeks of paid leave**.
- Maternity leave **cannot be combined with notice period** during resignation.
- The employee may apply for an **additional 4 weeks of unpaid leave** after the paid period with medical justification.

**Procedure:**
- Employee must notify HR and manager **at least 8 weeks before** the expected date of delivery.
- Submission of a **medical certificate** confirming the expected due date is mandatory.
- A **return-to-work plan** must be discussed with HR at least 2 weeks before the end of maternity leave.
- Upon return, the employee is **guaranteed the same or equivalent role**.

---

### 1.5 Paternity Leave

**Entitlement:**
- Male employees with **3 or more months of service** are entitled to **5 days of paid paternity leave** to be taken within **30 days of the child's birth or adoption**.
- Paternity leave is available for the **first two births** only.
- It can be taken in one block or split into no more than **two periods**.

---

### 1.6 Bereavement Leave

**Entitlement:**
- Employees are entitled to paid bereavement leave upon the death of an immediate family member:

| Relationship | Leave Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Spouse / Child | 5 working days |
| Parent / Parent-in-law | 4 working days |
| Sibling / Grandparent | 3 working days |
| Close relative (uncle, aunt, etc.) | 1 working day |

- Additional unpaid leave may be granted by the manager on a case-by-case basis.
- Proof such as a **death certificate or obituary notice** may be requested by HR.

---

### 1.7 Public Holidays

- NovaTech observes **12 mandatory public holidays** per year as declared by the national government.
- A **floating holiday list** of 3 optional days is published each January; employees may choose any 3 from this list.
- Employees required to work on a public holiday will receive **double pay** or a **compensatory day off** within 30 days.

---

### 1.8 Leave Without Pay (LWP)

- An employee who has exhausted all applicable leave may apply for LWP.
- **Manager and HR Head approval** are both required.
- LWP beyond **30 continuous days** may affect **annual increment eligibility**.
- LWP does not count toward **service tenure** for gratuity calculations.

## 2. ATTENDANCE & WORKING HOURS

---

### 2.1 Standard Working Hours

- The standard workweek at NovaTech is **Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM** (inclusive of a 1-hour lunch break).
- Total working hours: **8 hours per day / 40 hours per week**.
- Employees in customer-facing or operational roles may be assigned **shift timings** that differ from the standard schedule.

### 2.2 Flexible Working Hours (Flexi-Time)

- Eligible employees (confirmed, non-shift) may work under a **flexi-time arrangement** — core hours are **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM**, during which presence is mandatory.
- Flexi-time must be pre-approved by the manager and HR.
- Flexi-time does not reduce total required hours; the full **8 hours must still be completed**.

### 2.3 Late Arrival Policy

- Employees are expected to be at their workstation and productive by **9:00 AM**.
- A **grace period of 10 minutes** is allowed.
- Arrivals between **9:11 AM – 9:30 AM**: Noted as a late mark.
- **3 late marks in a calendar month** will result in **half a day's leave** being deducted.
- **6 or more late marks** in a month will trigger a formal discussion with the manager and HR.
- Habitual late arrivals (3+ months with consistent late marks) may lead to **disciplinary action**.

### 2.4 Overtime Policy

- Overtime is compensated for **non-managerial employees** at **1.5× the hourly rate**.
- Overtime must be **pre-approved** by the department head in writing.
- Managerial-level employees and above are expected to manage their time and are **not eligible for overtime pay**.
- A maximum of **10 overtime hours per week** is permitted without special HR approval.

## 3. CODE OF CONDUCT

---

### 3.1 Professional Behavior

All employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with **respect and professionalism** at all times.
- Maintain a **positive, collaborative, and inclusive** work environment.
- Avoid engaging in gossip, rumors, or politically divisive conversations in the workplace.
- Represent NovaTech's values in all professional and semi-professional settings.

### 3.2 Conflicts of Interest

- Employees must **disclose in writing** to HR any personal, financial, or professional interest that may conflict with company interests.
- Employees **may not hold any secondary employment** without prior written approval from HR and their department head.
- Immediate family members employed at competing firms must be disclosed at the time of joining or as soon as the conflict arises.

### 3.3 Gifts & Hospitality

- Employees may not accept gifts valued at more than **LKR 5,000** (or equivalent) from clients, vendors, or external parties.
- Gifts exceeding this threshold must be **surrendered to HR**, which may redistribute them or donate them to charity.
- Accepting cash, gift cards, or any form of monetary benefit from external parties is **strictly prohibited**.
- Offering bribes or kickbacks on behalf of or to NovaTech is grounds for **immediate termination**.

### 3.4 Social Media Policy

- Employees must not post **confidential or proprietary information** about NovaTech on social media.
- Employees representing NovaTech officially on social platforms must obtain **prior approval** from the Communications team.
- Derogatory or defamatory comments about colleagues, clients, or the company on any platform — even personal accounts — may result in **disciplinary action**.

## 4. DRESS CODE POLICY

---

### 4.1 Standard Dress Code (Monday–Thursday)

- **Business Casual** is the standard for office days.
- Acceptable: Collared shirts, blouses, trousers, formal skirts, chinos, formal shoes, loafers.
- Not acceptable: Ripped jeans, sleeveless tops (without a jacket), flip-flops, overly revealing clothing, clothing with offensive slogans.

### 4.2 Casual Fridays

- **Smart Casual** is permitted every Friday.
- Acceptable: Clean, neat jeans (no rips), polo shirts, casual shirts, sneakers.
- Not acceptable: Shorts, gym wear, loungewear, graphic tees with offensive content.

### 4.3 Client-Facing Days

- On days with **client meetings or presentations**, employees are expected to dress in **full Business Formal** regardless of the day of the week.
- Managers will notify their teams at least **24 hours in advance** of client visits.

### 4.4 Violations

- First violation: Verbal reminder from manager.
- Second violation: Written warning placed in employee file.
- Third violation: HR counseling session and written warning.

## 5. REMOTE WORK & WORK FROM HOME (WFH) POLICY

---

### 5.1 Eligibility

- Employees who have **completed their probation period (90 days)** and are in good standing are eligible for WFH.
- Roles designated as **on-site mandatory** (e.g., IT support, reception, operations) are not eligible.

### 5.2 WFH Entitlement

- Eligible employees may work from home up to **2 days per week**.
- WFH is **not permitted on Mondays** (mandatory in-office day for team syncs).
- WFH days cannot be used as a substitute for sick leave or personal leave.

### 5.3 WFH Procedure

- Employees must **request WFH at least 1 day in advance** via the HR portal.
- Manager approval is required.
- During WFH, employees must:
  - Be **reachable via phone, email, and Slack** during core hours (10 AM – 4 PM).
  - Join all scheduled **video calls with camera on** unless technically infeasible.
  - Maintain the same **productivity standards** as in-office days.

### 5.4 Equipment & Security

- Employees using personal devices must install the company's **approved VPN and endpoint security software**.
- Confidential documents must **not be printed** at home or saved on personal drives.
- Any data breach resulting from WFH negligence is the **employee's responsibility**.

## 6. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

---

### 6.1 Performance Review Cycle

- NovaTech operates on a **bi-annual performance review cycle**:
  - **Mid-Year Review:** July (feedback and goal realignment)
  - **Annual Review:** December (ratings, increments, promotions)

### 6.2 Rating Scale

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 5 – Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all expectations |
| 4 – Exceeds Expectations | Frequently surpasses goals |
| 3 – Meets Expectations | Consistently delivers on all goals |
| 2 – Needs Improvement | Partially meets expectations |
| 1 – Unsatisfactory | Fails to meet minimum requirements |

### 6.3 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

- Employees rated **1 or 2** for two consecutive cycles will be placed on a **PIP for 90 days**.
- The PIP outlines specific, measurable targets the employee must achieve.
- A dedicated **HR business partner** and mentor will be assigned.
- Failure to meet PIP targets may result in **termination of employment**.

### 6.4 Increment Policy

- Annual increments are based on performance ratings and budget availability:
  - Rating 5: **15–20% increment**
  - Rating 4: **10–14% increment**
  - Rating 3: **5–9% increment**
  - Rating 2: **0–4% increment**
  - Rating 1: **No increment; PIP initiated**

## 7. COMPENSATION & PAYROLL

---

### 7.1 Salary Payment

- Salaries are processed on the **last working day of each month** via direct bank transfer.
- Salary slips are available on the **employee self-service portal** by the 1st of the following month.
- Any payroll discrepancy must be reported to HR within **7 working days** of receiving the salary slip.

### 7.2 Salary Components

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Basic Salary | 50% of CTC |
| Housing Allowance | 20% of Basic |
| Transport Allowance | LKR 5,000 fixed |
| Medical Allowance | LKR 3,000 fixed |
| Performance Bonus | Up to 15% of annual CTC (at year-end) |

### 7.3 Statutory Deductions

- EPF (Employee Provident Fund): **8% employee contribution, 12% employer contribution**
- ETF (Employee Trust Fund): **3% employer contribution**
- PAYE Tax: As per prevailing government tax slabs

### 7.4 Bonus Policy

- An **annual performance bonus** is paid in January based on December review ratings and company profitability.
- Employees who joined after **September 1** of the same year receive a **pro-rated bonus**.
- Employees on active **disciplinary action** at the time of bonus payout are **not eligible**.

## 9. RECRUITMENT & ONBOARDING

---

### 9.1 Recruitment Policy

- All open positions must receive **internal job posting** visibility for **7 days** before external advertising.
- Employee referrals are encouraged; a **referral bonus of LKR 25,000** is paid to the referring employee if the referred candidate completes **3 months of service**.
- Candidates must complete: **Aptitude Test → Technical Interview → HR Interview → Background Check** before an offer is extended.

### 9.2 Offer & Joining

- Offer letters are valid for **7 days** from issuance.
- New hires must submit original documents (National ID, educational certificates, previous experience letters) on **Day 1**.
- Failure to submit valid documents within **5 working days** may result in **offer withdrawal**.

### 9.3 Probation Period

- All new hires undergo a **90-day probation period**.
- Performance is reviewed at **30 days, 60 days, and 90 days**.
- The company may extend probation by **up to an additional 60 days** if performance is not satisfactory.
- During probation, employees are **not eligible** for annual leave, annual bonus, or WFH.
- Either party may terminate employment during probation with **7 days' written notice**.

### 9.4 Onboarding

- New employees complete a **structured 2-week onboarding program** covering:
  - Company culture, values, and history
  - IT setup and tools training
  - Department orientation
  - Compliance and policy training
  - Introduction to key stakeholders

## 10. RESIGNATION & OFFBOARDING

---

### 10.1 Notice Period

| Level | Notice Period |
|-------|--------------|
| Junior (G1–G3) | 1 month |
| Mid-Level (G4–G6) | 2 months |
| Senior & Above (G7+) | 3 months |

- Notice period begins on the **date the written resignation is acknowledged by HR**.
- Notice can be served in full or **bought out** by either the employee or the company at the basic salary rate.

### 10.2 Resignation Procedure

1. Employee submits **written resignation** to their direct manager.
2. Manager acknowledges and forwards to HR within **24 hours**.
3. HR schedules **exit interview** within 5 working days.
4. Employee completes **knowledge transfer** and handover document.
5. IT, Finance, and Admin conduct **clearance sign-off**.
6. **Experience letter and relieving letter** issued on or before the last working day.
7. **Full & Final settlement** processed within **30 working days** of the last working day.

### 10.3 Abandonment of Employment

- An employee absent for **more than 5 consecutive working days** without any communication or approved leave will be considered to have **abandoned their employment**.
- HR will attempt to contact the employee via phone, email, and registered mail before issuing a formal abandonment notice.

## 11. GRIEVANCE & DISCIPLINARY POLICY

---

### 11.1 Grievance Procedure

**Step 1 – Informal Resolution:**
The employee discusses the issue directly with their immediate manager. A resolution should be reached within **5 working days**.

**Step 2 – Formal Complaint:**
If unresolved, the employee submits a **written grievance form** to HR. HR acknowledges receipt within **2 working days** and initiates an investigation.

**Step 3 – HR Investigation & Mediation:**
HR facilitates a **mediation session** between parties. A written resolution is issued within **10 working days**.

**Step 4 – Escalation:**
If dissatisfied, the employee may escalate to the **HR Director** within 5 working days of receiving the Step 3 decision.

**Step 5 – Final Escalation:**
Unresolved matters may be escalated to the **CEO's office**, whose decision is final and binding.

### 11.2 Disciplinary Actions

| Severity | Examples | Action |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Minor | Repeated lateness, dress code violations | Verbal warning → Written warning |
| Moderate | Insubordination, unauthorized absence, misuse of resources | Written warning → Final warning |
| Serious | Harassment, fraud, data breach, violence | Final warning → Suspension → Termination |
| Gross Misconduct | Theft, criminal activity, sexual harassment | Immediate termination |

- Employees under investigation have the right to be **accompanied by a colleague or union representative** during formal proceedings.
- All disciplinary records are maintained in the employee's file for **3 years**.

## 12. ANTI-HARASSMENT & NON-DISCRIMINATION POLICY

---

### 12.1 Policy Statement

NovaTech Solutions is committed to providing a workplace **free of discrimination, harassment, and bullying** of any kind.

### 12.2 Protected Characteristics

No employee shall be discriminated against on the basis of:
- Gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- Race, ethnicity, national origin, or religion
- Age, disability, or marital status
- Pregnancy or parental status
- Political views or social background

### 12.3 What Constitutes Harassment

- **Verbal:** Insults, slurs, offensive jokes, derogatory comments
- **Physical:** Unwanted touching, invasion of personal space, intimidation
- **Visual:** Displaying offensive material, inappropriate images
- **Digital:** Sending threatening or explicit messages, cyberbullying
- **Sexual:** Any unwanted sexual attention, quid-pro-quo advances, or creating a hostile environment

### 12.4 Reporting & Consequences

- Reports can be made to the **direct HR business partner** or through the **anonymous ethics hotline**.
- All complaints will be treated with **complete confidentiality**.
- Retaliation against a complainant is **strictly prohibited** and will itself be treated as misconduct.
- Substantiated complaints of harassment will result in **disciplinary action up to and including termination**.

## 13. DATA PRIVACY & CONFIDENTIALITY

---

### 13.1 Confidential Information

Employees must **not disclose, share, or misuse**:
- Client data, contracts, or personally identifiable information (PII)
- Financial data, forecasts, or strategic plans
- Proprietary source code, algorithms, or product designs
- Employee salaries, personal data, or HR records

### 13.2 Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

- All employees sign an **NDA at the time of joining**.
- NDA obligations **survive termination of employment** for a period of **2 years**.
- Breach of NDA may result in **legal action and financial liability**.

### 13.3 Data Handling Guidelines

- Customer data must only be accessed on **company-issued, secure devices**.
- Data must **never be transferred to personal email or storage** (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.).
- Employees must report **suspected data breaches immediately** to the IT Security team.

## 14. IT & TECHNOLOGY USAGE POLICY

---

### 14.1 Acceptable Use

Company IT assets (laptops, phones, software, network) are provided **solely for business use**.
Personal use is permitted only to a **limited and reasonable extent** that does not interfere with productivity.

### 14.2 Prohibited Activities

- Installing unlicensed or unauthorized software
- Accessing pirated content, gambling, or adult sites on company networks
- Using company assets to conduct a personal business
- Sharing company Wi-Fi credentials with third parties
- Attempting to bypass firewall or security systems

### 14.3 Password & Access Policy

- Passwords must be at least **12 characters**, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
- Passwords must be changed **every 90 days**.
- Employees must **never share login credentials** with anyone, including IT staff.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is **mandatory** for all company systems.

### 14.4 Device Policy

- Company devices lost or stolen must be **reported to IT within 1 hour** of discovery.
- IT has the right to **remotely wipe devices** upon loss or resignation.
- Employees leaving the company must **return all IT assets on the last working day**.

## 15. HEALTH, SAFETY & WELLNESS

---

### 15.1 Workplace Safety

- All employees are responsible for **maintaining a safe and clean** work environment.
- Safety incidents must be **reported immediately** to Facilities and HR.
- Emergency evacuation procedures are posted at **all exits**; employees must participate in **annual fire drills**.

### 15.2 Medical Insurance

- NovaTech provides **group health insurance** for all permanent employees from Day 1.
- Coverage includes:
  - **Employee:** LKR 500,000 annual inpatient cover
  - **Spouse:** LKR 300,000 annual inpatient cover
  - **Up to 2 dependent children:** LKR 150,000 each
- Dental and optical coverage is available as an **optional top-up** at the employee's cost.
- Insurance cards are issued within **30 days of joining**.

### 15.3 Employee Wellness Program

- NovaTech offers the following wellness benefits:
  - **Annual health check-up** (covered by company) for all employees
  - **Gym membership subsidy** of LKR 2,000/month
  - **Mental health support:** 6 free sessions per year with a company-empaneled therapist (fully confidential)
  - **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** 24/7 helpline for personal, financial, and mental health support

### 15.4 Substance Abuse Policy

- NovaTech maintains a **zero-tolerance policy** for being under the influence of alcohol or illegal substances during work hours or on company premises.
- Employees who voluntarily seek help for substance dependency will be **supported through the EAP** without punitive action, provided they have not caused harm to others.
- Employees found under the influence will be **immediately sent home** and subject to disciplinary review.
