ABOUT NEXACORE TECHNOLOGIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NexaCore Technologies is a mid-size IT services company headquartered in Austin, Texas, with regional offices in Chicago, New York, and Bangalore. Founded in 2011, we serve clients across healthcare, financial services, retail, and government sectors. Our core service lines include managed IT services, cloud migration, cybersecurity consulting, custom software development, and enterprise support operations.

As of 2024, NexaCore employs approximately 1,200 full-time staff globally, with 780 based in the United States.

SECTION 1: EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 Equal Opportunity Employment
NexaCore Technologies is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, disability, age, or veteran status. All hiring, promotion, compensation, and termination decisions are made based on merit and business need.

1.2 Employment Categories
- Full-Time Employee: Regularly scheduled to work 40 hours per week. Eligible for full benefits.
- Part-Time Employee: Scheduled for fewer than 30 hours per week. Limited benefits eligibility.
- Contract/Temporary Employee: Engaged for a defined period or project. Not eligible for standard benefits unless stated in contract.
- Intern: Student or recent graduate engaged for a fixed period. Supervised learning role.

1.3 Work Hours and Flexibility
Standard work hours are 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday. NexaCore supports hybrid work arrangements where roles permit. Employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM for meetings and collaboration. Remote work beyond the standard hybrid arrangement requires VP-level approval.

1.4 Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to:
- Behave professionally and respectfully toward colleagues, clients, and vendors
- Protect confidential information belonging to NexaCore and its clients
- Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any potential conflicts promptly
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and internal policies
- Not engage in harassment, bullying, or discriminatory behavior of any kind

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

1.5 Dress Code
NexaCore maintains a business casual dress code in office environments. When visiting client sites, employees are expected to dress in full business attire unless the client specifies otherwise. Branded NexaCore attire is permitted on Fridays.

SECTION 2: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Payroll
Employees are paid on a bi-weekly schedule (every other Friday). Direct deposit is required for all employees. Pay stubs are accessible through the HR portal at hr.nexacore.internal.

2.2 Health Benefits
NexaCore provides health, dental, and vision insurance through BlueCross BlueShield. Enrollment is available during onboarding and annually during the Open Enrollment period each November. Employees may cover eligible dependents at an additional cost. Details on plan tiers (Standard, Plus, Premium) are available in the Benefits Guide published by HR.

2.3 Retirement Plan
NexaCore offers a 401(k) plan through Fidelity Investments. The company matches 50% of employee contributions up to 6% of base salary. Employees are eligible to enroll after completing 90 days of employment. Vesting occurs over a 3-year schedule.

2.4 Performance Reviews and Raises
Performance reviews are conducted annually in Q4 (October–November). Merit-based salary increases, if applicable, take effect on January 1 of the following year. Mid-year reviews may be conducted for employees on performance improvement plans or recently promoted staff.

2.5 Bonuses
Eligible employees may receive an annual performance bonus based on individual ratings and company financial performance. Bonus eligibility criteria and targets are communicated each year by Finance in Q3. Contract employees are not eligible for bonuses unless explicitly stated in their agreements.

SECTION 3: LEAVE AND TIME OFF
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
NexaCore uses a unified PTO policy combining vacation, personal days, and non-illness-related absences:
- 0–2 years of service: 15 days per year
- 3–5 years of service: 18 days per year
- 6+ years of service: 22 days per year

PTO must be approved by your direct manager at least 5 business days in advance for planned leave. PTO accrues monthly and unused PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year.

3.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 sick days per calendar year, separate from PTO. Sick days do not carry over. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required.

3.3 Public Holidays
NexaCore observes 11 federal holidays per year. A full list is published each December by HR for the following year. Employees required to work on public holidays will receive compensatory time off.

3.4 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave must be taken within 6 months of the qualifying event.

3.5 Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). 2 days are granted for extended family members (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle).

3.6 Unpaid Leave
Employees may request unpaid leave of absence for personal reasons. Requests must be submitted in writing to HR and are subject to approval based on business needs. Unpaid leave does not affect benefits but may impact bonus eligibility.

SECTION 4: WORKPLACE POLICIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Anti-Harassment Policy
NexaCore has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment of any kind — sexual, racial, religious, or otherwise. Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it to HR or use the anonymous reporting hotline at 1-800-NEX-SAFE. All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially.

4.2 Substance Use
NexaCore maintains a drug-free workplace. Use, possession, or distribution of illegal substances on company premises or during work hours is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate termination. Employees taking prescription medication that may impact performance should notify HR.

4.3 Use of Company Property
Company-issued devices, software, and networks are intended for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted, but employees should have no expectation of privacy on company systems. Unauthorized software installation or data access is prohibited.

4.4 Confidentiality and NDA
All employees are required to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) as a condition of employment. Client data, internal strategies, pricing, and proprietary technologies must not be shared externally without explicit authorization. This obligation continues after termination.

4.5 Social Media
Employees must not post confidential company or client information on social media. Personal social media use is permitted during non-work hours. Employees representing NexaCore in a professional capacity online must comply with the Communications and Brand Guidelines issued by Marketing.

SECTION 5: SEPARATION FROM EMPLOYMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Resignation
Employees are expected to provide a minimum of 2 weeks' written notice of resignation. Senior roles (Manager and above) are expected to provide 4 weeks' notice. During the notice period, employees are expected to maintain full duties and assist in transition.

5.2 Termination
NexaCore reserves the right to terminate employment at any time. In cases of misconduct, termination may be immediate without notice. In cases of layoffs or restructuring, employees will receive at least 2 weeks' notice or equivalent pay.

5.3 Exit Process
Upon separation, employees must return all company property, complete an exit interview with HR, and have their final paycheck issued within 3 business days as per applicable state law.
