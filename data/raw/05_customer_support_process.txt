1. OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NexaCore Technologies provides tiered customer support services to all clients under active contracts. Our support model is designed to deliver fast, consistent, and measurable resolution of client issues across our managed IT, cloud, software, and cybersecurity service lines.

This document outlines the intake, triage, escalation, and resolution processes used by our support teams.

2. SUPPORT TIERS AND SLAs
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NexaCore clients are assigned to a support tier based on their contract:

TIER 1 — BASIC SUPPORT
- Available: Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM CT
- Channels: Email, support portal
- First Response SLA: 8 business hours
- Resolution SLA: 5 business days for standard issues

TIER 2 — PROFESSIONAL SUPPORT
- Available: Mon–Fri, 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM CT
- Channels: Email, support portal, phone
- First Response SLA: 4 business hours
- Resolution SLA: 3 business days for standard issues
- Includes: Dedicated support engineer

TIER 3 — ENTERPRISE SUPPORT
- Available: 24/7/365
- Channels: Email, portal, phone, dedicated Slack channel
- First Response SLA: 1 hour (P1 issues), 4 hours (P2), 8 hours (P3/P4)
- Resolution SLA: Defined per client SLA agreement
- Includes: Named Client Success Manager (CSM), quarterly business reviews

Note: SLA timers pause on weekends and holidays for Tier 1 and Tier 2 clients.

3. ISSUE PRIORITY DEFINITIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

P1 — CRITICAL
Definition: Complete service outage or data breach affecting the client's core operations. No workaround available.
Examples: Production system down, security breach, data loss, complete API failure
Response: Immediate — on-call engineer paged within 15 minutes

P2 — HIGH
Definition: Major feature or service degraded; significant business impact. Limited workaround available.
Examples: Degraded performance affecting >50% of users, partial outage, failed deployments
Response: Within 1–4 hours depending on tier

P3 — MEDIUM
Definition: Non-critical issue with moderate business impact. Workaround available.
Examples: Single feature malfunctioning, intermittent errors, reporting discrepancies
Response: Within 4–8 business hours

P4 — LOW
Definition: Minor issue or informational request. No business impact.
Examples: UI cosmetic issue, documentation request, general how-to questions
Response: Within 1–5 business days

4. TICKET INTAKE AND HANDLING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Intake Channels
Clients may submit support requests via:
- Support Portal: support.nexacore.com (all tiers)
- Email: support@nexacore.com (all tiers)
- Phone: 1-800-NEX-HELP (Tier 2 and above)
- Dedicated Slack Channel (Tier 3 only)

All tickets are logged in Zendesk and assigned a unique ticket number. Auto-acknowledgment is sent to the client immediately upon submission.

4.2 Triage
Tickets are triaged by the Tier 1 Support Analyst within the first response SLA. Triage involves:
- Validating the issue description
- Assigning priority (P1–P4) based on business impact assessment
- Tagging the relevant service domain (cloud, security, app, infrastructure)
- Escalating immediately if P1 or security-related

4.3 First Response
The first response must:
- Acknowledge the issue
- Confirm the assigned priority
- Provide an initial assessment or request additional information
- Set expectations on next steps and timeline

Do not close a ticket without a resolution confirmed by the client contact.

5. ESCALATION PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

L1 Analyst → L2 Engineer → L3 Specialist / Dev Team → Management Escalation

Escalation triggers:
- SLA breach imminent or occurring
- P1 issue not resolved within 2 hours
- Client requests escalation
- Issue requires code change or infrastructure modification
- Security or compliance concern identified

For P1 issues, the following must be notified:
- On-call engineer (PagerDuty)
- Support Manager
- Client Success Manager (for Tier 3 clients)
- VP of Engineering (if infrastructure-wide impact)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- For P1 issues: Update client every 30 minutes until resolved
- For P2 issues: Update client every 2 hours
- For P3/P4: Update when status changes or when requested
- All communication must be professional, empathetic, and solution-focused
- Do not speculate on root cause until confirmed by technical investigation
- Do not promise a fix timeline unless approved by the engineering lead

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. RESOLUTION AND CLOSURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A ticket may be resolved when:
- The reported issue has been fixed and confirmed by the support engineer
- The client confirms resolution or does not respond within 48 hours of the resolution notice

Upon closure:
- A resolution summary must be added to the Zendesk ticket
- CSAT survey is automatically sent to the client contact
- For P1/P2 issues: A Root Cause Analysis (RCA) report must be completed within 5 business days and shared with the client
