PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This policy outlines the types of leave available to NexaCore employees, eligibility requirements, and procedures for requesting leave. This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Contractors and temporary staff are not covered under this policy unless explicitly stated in their contracts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 Accrual Schedule
PTO accrues monthly based on tenure:
- 0–2 years: 15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
- 3–5 years: 18 days per year (1.5 days/month)
- 6+ years: 22 days per year (~1.83 days/month)

Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis based on scheduled hours.

1.2 PTO Carryover
Employees may carry over a maximum of 10 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. PTO above this limit is forfeited at year-end. Employees are encouraged to plan and use their PTO throughout the year.

1.3 PTO Payout
PTO is not paid out upon resignation or termination in most U.S. states. However, employees in California, Colorado, Illinois, and New York are entitled to payout of accrued unused PTO as required by state law. HR will confirm payout eligibility based on work location.

1.4 Requesting PTO
- Submit requests via Workday (workday.nexacore.com)
- Minimum notice: 5 business days for planned leave
- Requests over 10 consecutive days require manager and HR approval
- PTO may be denied during high-demand project periods at manager discretion

2. SICK LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Employees receive 10 sick days per calendar year. Sick leave:
- Does not accrue — it is granted in full at the start of each calendar year
- Does not carry over to the following year
- Is separate from PTO and does not affect the PTO balance

For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate may be required. For chronic conditions requiring recurring leave, employees should contact HR to discuss accommodations.

Note: Sick leave may be used for personal illness, caring for an immediate family member who is ill, or medical appointments.

3. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NexaCore observes 11 federal public holidays annually. The full calendar for the year is published each December by HR. Employees required to work on a public holiday will receive:
- Compensatory day off to be taken within 30 days, OR
- If compensatory time is not feasible, an additional day's pay (requires manager and HR approval)

Employees in the Bangalore office follow a separate holiday calendar combining Indian national holidays and a selection of U.S. holidays for global collaboration days.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. PARENTAL LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Eligibility
Available to employees who have completed at least 6 months of employment.

4.2 Entitlements
- Primary caregiver: 12 weeks fully paid
- Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks fully paid

Parental leave may be taken following:
- Birth of a child
- Adoption of a child under 18
- Foster placement of a child

Leave must commence within 6 months of the qualifying event. It can be taken in one block or split into two periods (subject to manager and HR approval).

4.3 State-Specific Entitlements
Employees in states with mandatory paid family leave programs (e.g., California, New York, New Jersey, Washington) may be entitled to additional benefits through state programs. HR will coordinate to maximize benefits.

4.4 Return to Work
Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to return to the same or equivalent role. A return-to-work plan should be discussed with HR at least 4 weeks before the planned return date.

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 paid days
- Extended family (grandparent, in-laws, aunt, uncle, niece, nephew): 2 paid days
- Close personal friend or non-listed family: Up to 1 day at manager discretion

Additional unpaid leave may be approved by HR in exceptional circumstances. Bereavement leave does not require advance notice and can be taken immediately.

6. JURY DUTY AND CIVIC LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Employees summoned for jury duty or subpoenaed to appear as witnesses in court will receive their full salary for the duration of their absence. Employees must notify their manager and HR as soon as they receive a jury summons and provide a copy of the summons. If released from jury duty before 3:00 PM, employees are expected to return to work for the remainder of the day where practicable.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. MILITARY LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NexaCore complies with the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA). Employees called to active military duty are entitled to unpaid military leave with reemployment rights upon return. In cases of short-term military training (≤30 days), NexaCore will pay the difference between military pay and regular salary.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
8. UNPAID LEAVE OF ABSENCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Employees may request unpaid personal leave for situations not covered by other leave types. Requests:
- Must be submitted in writing to HR with reason and expected duration
- Are subject to business need approval
- Cannot exceed 3 months without VP and HR Director approval
- Benefits continue during unpaid leave (employee pays full premium during extended leave)
- Extended leave may affect bonus eligibility for the year

9. LEAVE ABUSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NexaCore monitors leave usage patterns to identify potential misuse. Patterns such as repeated sick leave adjacent to weekends or holidays, or frequent unplanned absences, may trigger a conversation with HR. Leave abuse may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
