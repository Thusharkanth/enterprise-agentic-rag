1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This policy defines how NexaCore Technologies manages data assets throughout their lifecycle — from creation and classification to storage, use, sharing, and disposal. Proper data governance is essential to meeting our regulatory obligations and maintaining client trust.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. DATA CLASSIFICATION FRAMEWORK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NexaCore classifies all data into four tiers. Every data asset must be classified at the time of creation or acquisition.

TIER 1 — PUBLIC
Description: Information intended for public access.
Examples: Marketing materials, public website content, press releases, published case studies.
Handling: No restrictions on access or distribution.

TIER 2 — INTERNAL
Description: General business information not meant for public release but not sensitive.
Examples: Internal memos, meeting notes, process documentation, organizational charts.
Handling: Accessible to all employees. Do not share externally without approval.

TIER 3 — CONFIDENTIAL
Description: Sensitive business or client information that could cause harm if disclosed.
Examples: Client contracts, financial projections, employee compensation data, vendor agreements, unreleased product roadmaps, client infrastructure details.
Handling:
- Access on need-to-know basis only
- Stored only in approved encrypted systems
- Requires DPA when shared with third-party AI tools or vendors
- Must not be emailed externally without encryption

TIER 4 — RESTRICTED
Description: Highest sensitivity. Legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations require strict protection.
Examples:
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII): Name + email, SSN, government ID, financial account numbers
- Protected Health Information (PHI): Any health records under HIPAA scope
- Payment Card Industry (PCI) data: Card numbers, CVV, cardholder data
- Government contract data: Classified or CUI (Controlled Unclassified Information)
- Trade secrets and proprietary source code
Handling:
- Access strictly limited and logged
- Must be stored in dedicated, compliance-certified systems
- Transmission only via encrypted secure file transfer (not email)
- Must never be entered into AI tools of any kind
- Retention limited by regulation; disposal requires certificate of destruction

3. DATA OWNERSHIP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Each data asset must have an assigned Data Owner — a department head or senior leader responsible for:
- Determining and reviewing classification
- Approving access requests
- Ensuring data is handled in accordance with this policy
- Conducting annual data asset reviews

Data Stewards (typically managers or senior individual contributors) assist the Data Owner with day-to-day governance tasks.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. DATA RETENTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Retention schedules are defined by data type and regulatory requirement:

| Data Type                        | Retention Period      | Notes                            |
|----------------------------------|-----------------------|----------------------------------|
| Employee records                 | 7 years post-departure| Per FLSA and state requirements  |
| Client contracts                 | 10 years post-expiry  | Legal hold if in litigation      |
| Financial records                | 7 years               | IRS / SOX requirements           |
| Security logs / audit trails     | 24 months             | SOC 2 requirement                |
| Support tickets                  | 3 years               | Per client SLA agreements        |
| PHI (healthcare client data)     | 6 years minimum       | HIPAA requirement                |
| PCI data (cardholder)            | Do not retain         | Tokenize immediately             |
| Marketing / general comms        | 2 years               | No regulatory requirement        |

Data should not be retained beyond its required retention period. Disposal must follow the secure disposal process defined in Section 6.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. DATA ACCESS AND SHARING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Internal Access
Access to Tier 3 and Tier 4 data must be requested through the IT helpdesk and approved by the data owner. Access logs are reviewed quarterly by the Security team.

5.2 External Sharing
Tier 3 data shared externally (with clients, vendors, or partners) requires:
- A signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA) or NDA in place
- Encrypted transmission
- Data Owner approval

Tier 4 data must not be shared externally except where legally required and only with explicit Legal and CISO approval.

5.3 Cross-Border Data Transfer
Transfer of personal data across borders (e.g., from EU to US) must comply with applicable transfer mechanisms (Standard Contractual Clauses for GDPR-scope data). Contact Legal before initiating any cross-border data transfer involving Tier 3/4 data.

6. DATA DISPOSAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

When data reaches end of retention or is no longer needed:
- Digital data: Cryptographic erasure or secure deletion (DoD 5220.22-M standard or equivalent)
- Physical media: Shredding via certified destruction vendor (IT to coordinate)
- Cloud data: Confirm deletion through provider's deletion API + log retention confirmation
- Certificate of Destruction is required for Tier 4 data disposal

7. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

In the event of a data breach involving Tier 3 or Tier 4 data:
- Internal notification: Security team within 1 hour of discovery
- Legal & Compliance: Within 2 hours
- Client notification: Per contract terms (usually 24–72 hours)
- Regulatory notification: GDPR — 72 hours to supervisory authority; HIPAA — 60 days to HHS; State breach laws vary

Full breach response procedure: See Incident Management Workflow (INC-SOP-003).

8. TRAINING AND AWARENESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All employees must complete the annual Data Privacy and Classification training module in the LMS. Employees handling Tier 3/4 data must complete additional role-specific training. Failure to complete training results in access restrictions until completion.
