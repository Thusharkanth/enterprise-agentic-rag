1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This policy governs the procurement, tracking, maintenance, and disposal of IT hardware and software assets at NexaCore Technologies. Proper asset management ensures security, compliance, and cost efficiency.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. HARDWARE PROCUREMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Standard Equipment
All hardware purchases must go through the IT procurement process. Standard equipment issued to employees:

Engineering / Technical Roles:
- MacBook Pro 14" (M3, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) — standard issue
- MacBook Pro 16" (M3 Pro, 32GB RAM) — for ML/data engineering roles or on request with manager approval

Non-Technical Roles:
- Dell Latitude 5540 (i5, 16GB RAM, 256GB SSD)
- Lenovo ThinkPad E14 (alternative for budget-constrained hiring classes)

Peripherals (one bundle per full-time employee):
- 27" monitor (Dell or LG — subject to availability)
- Keyboard and mouse
- USB-C hub / docking station
- Webcam (if not built-in)

Additional peripherals may be requested via IT helpdesk with manager approval.

2.2 Procurement Process
1. Employee or manager submits IT Request via help.nexacore.internal
2. IT reviews request against standard specs and budget
3. Requests within standard spec: Auto-approved, ordered within 3 business days
4. Requests outside standard spec: Requires IT Manager + Finance approval
5. Equipment delivered to employee within 7–10 business days (US), 14–21 days (Bangalore)

Hardware must never be purchased directly by employees and expensed — all procurement goes through IT.

2.3 Budget
Annual hardware refresh budget is set each January by Finance in coordination with IT. Priority for refresh is given to devices over 3 years old. Devices that fail outside the refresh cycle are replaced on a priority basis.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. SOFTWARE PROCUREMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Approved Software List
IT maintains an Approved Software List at it.nexacore.internal/approved-software. Software on this list can be installed by the employee or is pre-installed on managed devices.

3.2 Requesting New Software
To request software not on the approved list:
1. Submit a Software Approval Request via IT helpdesk
2. Include: software name, version, purpose, cost, and vendor security questionnaire (template available on IT portal)
3. IT Security reviews for security risk
4. Legal reviews licensing and DPA requirements
5. Finance approves budget impact
6. Decision within 10 business days

Requests for AI tools must additionally be reviewed by the AI Governance Committee (see AI Policy).

3.3 SaaS Tools
All SaaS subscriptions must go through the procurement process regardless of cost. Employees may not sign up for SaaS tools using a company credit card without prior approval. Shadow IT (unapproved SaaS usage) poses significant security and compliance risks.

Current SaaS catalog is managed in Zylo (it.nexacore.internal/saas-catalog).

4. ASSET TRACKING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All hardware assets are tracked in ServiceNow (IT Asset Management module). Every device is assigned:
- Asset tag (physical label)
- Serial number
- Assigned user
- Location
- Purchase date and warranty expiry

Employees must confirm their assigned assets in ServiceNow annually (reminder sent by IT in January). Discrepancies must be reported to IT immediately.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. DEVICE RETURN AND OFFBOARDING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

When an employee leaves NexaCore (regardless of reason):
- All company devices must be returned to IT within 5 business days of last day
- IT will ship a return kit to remote employees with prepaid label
- Devices will be wiped and re-provisioned or decommissioned
- Software licenses are de-provisioned same day as access termination

IT works closely with HR for all offboarding. If devices are not returned, NexaCore reserves the right to charge the employee for the depreciated value of the equipment.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. ASSET DISPOSAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Decommissioned devices are:
1. Wiped using NIST SP 800-88 compliant tools
2. Donated to partner refurbishment programs (if functional) or disposed via certified e-waste vendor
3. Certificate of Destruction obtained for devices containing sensitive data

Employees may not take home or keep decommissioned company equipment. Unauthorized retention is a policy violation.
