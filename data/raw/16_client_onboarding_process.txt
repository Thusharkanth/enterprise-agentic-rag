NEXACORE TECHNOLOGIES — CLIENT ONBOARDING PROCESS

Owner: Client Success & Sales Operations

1. OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This document describes the end-to-end process for onboarding a new client to NexaCore Technologies' services. A smooth onboarding sets the tone for the entire client relationship and directly impacts time-to-value, satisfaction, and retention.

Client onboarding typically spans 4–12 weeks depending on service complexity. This process applies to all new client engagements with an ACV (Annual Contract Value) of $50,000 or more. For smaller clients, a simplified onboarding process (see Appendix A — not yet written) applies.

2. PRE-ONBOARDING (CONTRACT CLOSE TO KICKOFF)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Immediately upon contract execution:

Sales → Client Success Handoff (within 24 hours of close):
- Account Executive sends internal handoff email to assigned CSM with:
  - Signed contract and SOW
  - Client background and stakeholder map
  - Agreed service scope and SLA tier
  - Known risks or sensitivities
  - Client's industry (healthcare, finance, etc.) for compliance awareness

CSM Actions (within 48 hours):
- Create client account in Zendesk (support system)
- Create client workspace in Confluence
- Open onboarding project in Jira (use template: CS-ONBOARD-TMPL)
- Schedule kickoff meeting (target: within 5 business days of contract close)
- Send welcome email to client primary contact

Legal/Compliance (within 5 business days):
- Confirm NDA and DPA are fully executed
- Flag any special compliance requirements (HIPAA BAA, PCI DSS, government clauses)
- Provide compliance checklist to CSM based on client industry

3. KICKOFF MEETING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The kickoff meeting is the first formal interaction between the NexaCore delivery team and the client. It sets expectations and aligns on goals.

Attendees (NexaCore): CSM, Delivery/Project Lead, relevant technical SME
Attendees (Client): Executive Sponsor, Primary POC, technical lead

Agenda:
1. Introductions and relationship overview
2. Confirm scope, deliverables, and timeline
3. Review SLA tier and support process
4. Establish communication cadence and escalation path
5. Identify first milestone and quick win
6. Agree on tools and channels (Slack shared channel, email, portal)
7. Next steps and action items

CSM documents the meeting in Confluence and sends a follow-up email with action items within 24 hours.

4. TECHNICAL ONBOARDING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Environment Setup
For managed cloud clients: NexaCore provisions a dedicated AWS or Azure account (depending on client preference) using our standard account vending machine process. Timeline: 5–10 business days.

For software delivery clients: Engineering team sets up project repos, CI/CD, and environments per the engagement SOW.

4.2 Access Provisioning
Client-specific access is granted after security review:
- Client technical staff receive access to relevant dashboards, repos, or environments
- NexaCore staff are granted least-privilege access to client environments
- All access is documented in the Vendor Register and reviewed quarterly

4.3 Integration Setup
For clients integrating with NexaCore APIs or platforms:
- API credentials issued via the secure client portal
- Integration kickoff call with NexaCore engineering lead
- API documentation shared (currently PDF via CSM — developer portal not yet live)
- Sandbox environment provided for testing (10 business days to provision)

4.4 Security Requirements for Healthcare Clients
Clients in the healthcare sector require additional steps:
- Signed HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) — must be in place before any data exchange
- PHI data must only be stored in HIPAA-compliant AWS or Azure configurations
- NexaCore security team conducts an environment readiness review before go-live

4.5 Security Requirements for Financial Clients
- PCI-DSS compliance scope defined — cardholder data must be tokenized
- Confirm client's own PCI compliance status
- NexaCore's scope of PCI responsibility documented in SOW

5. GO-LIVE AND HYPERCARE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Go-Live Readiness Checklist
Before any client go-live:
☐ All contractual deliverables in scope are complete or deferred with client sign-off
☐ SLAs are configured in Zendesk and monitoring tools
☐ Support contacts established on both sides
☐ Runbook for client environment documented
☐ Monitoring and alerting configured (Datadog dashboards and PagerDuty alerts)
☐ Client team trained on relevant tools/portals
☐ Go-live communication drafted and approved by client

5.2 Hypercare Period
For the first 30 days post-go-live, all clients receive hypercare:
- Enhanced monitoring: daily system health checks
- Weekly check-in calls with CSM
- Faster response times (treated as one tier above contracted SLA)
- Dedicated Slack channel for the hypercare period (Tier 3 Slack channel extended to other tiers)

5.3 Post-Hypercare Transition
After 30 days: Transition to standard operating cadence as defined in the SLA. CSM schedules a hypercare review meeting to confirm client satisfaction.

6. ONGOING RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Post-onboarding, the CSM manages the ongoing relationship:
- Monthly or quarterly business reviews (QBRs) based on tier and ACV
- NPS surveys sent quarterly
- Contract renewal tracking 90 days before expiry
- Expansion opportunities flagged to Sales
